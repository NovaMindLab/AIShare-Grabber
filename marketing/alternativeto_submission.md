### 4. 详细描述 (Long Description for AlternativeTo)

```markdown
ShareCLIP is a 100% private, open-source, local-first media powerhouse designed to replace cloud subscriptions (Google Photos, iCloud) and cable-based transfers. It seamlessly bridges Android, Windows, macOS, Linux, and Web browsers over high-speed local Wi-Fi and WebRTC.

### 🌟 Key Features:
1. **P2P Local-First Transfer (AirDrop Alternative)**:
   - Direct Wi-Fi Hotspot & WebRTC peer-to-peer sync between Android and PC/Mac.
   - Zero internet required, zero cables, zero data compression. Transfer gigabytes of 4K photos and videos in seconds.
   - Built-in pure-web browser client (WebShare): share files to any device without installing apps.

2. **100% On-Device AI Photo Search (No Cloud Privacy Risk)**:
   - Powered by Apple MobileCLIP and ONNX Runtime: search your entire offline photo library using natural language prompts (e.g. "sunset at beach", "dog playing with ball", "receipts and invoices").
   - Offline face recognition & clustering, EXIF geolocation interactive world map, and smart timeline virtual scrolling.

3. **Universal 4K Multi-Site Video Downloader (yt-dlp GUI)**:
   - High-speed video downloader supporting YouTube, Bilibili, Douyin/TikTok, Kuaishou, Twitter/X, Xiaohongshu, and 1000+ platforms.
   - Automatic DASH audio-video merging into pristine MP4 via FFmpeg and automatic cover thumbnail embedding.
   - Dedicated independent Electron HTML5 player window with always-on-top picture-in-picture, HTTP 206 streaming Range seeking, and keyboard shortcuts.

4. **AnimeGAN Video Studio**:
   - Convert ordinary real-life videos into gorgeous anime art styles (Hayao, Shinkai) fully offline on your GPU/CPU.
```
