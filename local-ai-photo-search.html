Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How does ShareCLIP perform AI search without internet?

ShareCLIP packages an optimized, quantized MobileCLIP2-S0 ONNX model directly inside the desktop and mobile binaries. It runs inference locally using CPU/GPU hardware acceleration.

Q2: Are my photos or biometric facial models uploaded anywhere?

Never. All vector embeddings, face landmarks, and metadata are saved strictly inside a local SQLite database on your device.

Q3: How many photos can ShareCLIP index?

ShareCLIP indexes tens of thousands of images seamlessly, utilizing lightweight 512-dimensional vector indexes and SIMD-accelerated cosine similarity.
